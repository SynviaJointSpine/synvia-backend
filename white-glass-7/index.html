The Lean Forward program

A plan for the part that keeps tripping you up.

Maybe you do well all day and snack through the evening. Maybe you lose weight, then regain it when life gets busy. We’ll talk through what you’ve tried, what gets in the way, and which options fit your goals and health history.

GLP‑1 options for appetite support

For eligible patients, prescription GLP‑1 medications can help reduce appetite and help you feel full. A clinician reviews your health history, current medications, and potential side effects before recommending an option.

A plan you can keep following

Talk through realistic changes to meals, activity, and routines. Ask how follow-up works, what to track, and how the team can help you adjust if you hit a plateau or have side effects.

Red light therapy and body contouring

Ask whether a light-based body contouring option makes sense for your goals. Some devices are intended for temporary changes in the appearance or circumference of a treated area. Weight management is a separate goal.

What happens on the call?

We’ll ask about your goals, explain the program and costs, and walk you through the next step if you’d like a clinical evaluation. You can also ask what options make sense if joint discomfort makes it harder to stay active.

Can I get started before Christmas?

Request a call to check availability and find out what a clinical evaluation would involve. We can talk through a realistic first milestone for you.

What about peptides for inflammation?

Tell us if joint discomfort or inflammation is affecting your activity. A clinician can help you evaluate the cause and discuss options, including what evidence and safety information are available for any peptide you’re considering.

Do I have to decide about medication on the call?

You can use the call to ask questions and learn what a clinical evaluation would involve. A prescription decision requires a clinician’s review.
